# Obvi Training

## Unreleased

- First version: a **Train plan** button on the class trainer that trains only the spells in your class's training
  plan. The first plan: Retribution Paladin.
- **The plan window** (`/otr`): every spell your trainer teaches, with a box per rank coloured by what the plan does
  with it, and what the plan costs against training everything.
- **Edit plans** in the window: click a spell's rule to change it; new plans, copies, and Reset for the built-in ones.
- **Export and import** plans as text, to share them.
- **Train plan asks first**: it lists every spell and rank it will train (also the higher ranks that open up as it
  trains the lower ones) with the costs and the total, and trains only that.
